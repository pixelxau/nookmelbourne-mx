Finding your bearings

If you know Mexico City

We have spent a bit of time in Mexico and CDMX and it's our favourite city in the world. Southbank, with its towers, museums, theatres and the river, feels like Roma/Condesa with bustling nightlife, bars, great food and lots of things to do.

While Docklands and the CBD are mainly offices, and lack the nightlife, Southbank is primarily a living area so has a great energy about it for a great vibe at night.

At night

Dinner by the river, until late

The riverside promenade is about 7 minutes' walk away. The Southgate restaurants open every day from 10am until late, and Arts Centre Melbourne is about 11 minutes away.

Ponyfish Island and Yarra Botanica are floating bars on the river. Hacienda, a Mexican restaurant at Southgate about 9 minutes away, keeps its bar open until 1am.

Every day

The supermarket is across the road

There is a supermarket across the road with great Latin and Mexican ingredients. Two Woolworths stores are about 4 minutes' walk away and an IGA about 5, while Aldi is about 10 minutes away and Coles about 12.

South Melbourne Market, which opened in 1867, is a short walk away and we can recommend the best places to get Mexican ingredients and tortillas so you don't miss home too much. It is the best market in Melbourne and has Latin and Mexican groceries.

Getting around

The tram, three minutes away

The nearest tram stop is Stop 116 City Road, about 3 minutes' walk away, served by multiple routes.

Flinders Street Station is about 10 minutes away.

In the building

Pool, gym and tennis court

The pool, gym and tennis court are for residents, and your access fob gets you in. After class, you can swim or train without leaving the building.

Questions

Southbank or Docklands?

Southbank. Docklands is mainly offices and quiet at night, while Southbank is where people live, with the river, bars and restaurants a few minutes' walk away.

How far is it from my university?

All the universities are an easy tram ride or a walk away.

How do I get there from the airport?

Take SkyBus from Melbourne Airport to Southern Cross Station, about 30 minutes, running from 4am to 1am. From there it is about 17 to 19 minutes in total, on tram 12 or 96 and then a short walk.

Is there a supermarket nearby?

Yes, there is a supermarket across the road with Latin and Mexican ingredients, and others about 4 or 5 minutes' walk away.

Can I get Mexican food in Melbourne?

Yes, Hacienda at Southgate is a Mexican restaurant about 9 minutes' walk away. But the best tortillas are actually at our home, and we invite all students to have a nice dinner with us, so you don't miss home too much. We are the best Mexican food cooks in Melbourne.
