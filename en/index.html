We rent whole furnished apartments on City Road, Southbank, in Melbourne, to one group at a time.

We have hosted over 300 students on exchange for over 10 years, and students from Mexico have spent multiple semesters with us.

The apartment

Room to live for the whole semester

Your apartment has bedrooms, a living room, a dining table and a kitchen for your group, with a large-screen TV, fast internet and great amenities in the building: a pool, a gym and a tennis court.

There is a full kitchen with a gas stove and dishwasher, and the apartment has a washing machine, a separate dryer and air conditioning.

  • A proper dining table
  • Separate living area with sofas
  • Full kitchen with gas stove
  • Washing machine and dryer
  • Large-screen TV
  • Fast internet

The area

The river, nightlife and restaurants, at your doorstep

Southbank is a part of Melbourne where people live, party and enjoy life like Roma/Condesa in the city. The riverside promenade is about 7 minutes' walk from the apartment, and the Southgate restaurants open every day until late. This is where you want to be.

We have all of our apartments in Southbank, which is the best location in Melbourne. Other locations such as Central downtown and Docklands are mostly offices which are quiet at night and have no nightlife.

For everyday shopping there is a supermarket across the road, and others about 4 or 5 minutes' walk away.

How it works

Everything sorted before you fly

  1. You message us

    Send us your semester dates on WhatsApp and tell us how many people are in your group.

  2. We reply

    We send you the apartment options for those dates and a quote.

  3. We talk on Zoom

    We have a Zoom meeting with you and answer all your questions about Melbourne.

  4. You book it

Your hosts

With you before and after you arrive

Before you book, we get on a Zoom call with your group to answer every question. When you arrive, we meet you and check you into your apartment.

We give you information on opening a bank account, and tell you where to shop and where to find Mexican ingredients and tortillas. And we invite you to dinner at our home.

Questions

Where should I live in Melbourne for a semester exchange?

In Southbank, by the river, where people live and go out at night. We rent whole furnished apartments there, on City Road, to groups of students. All the universities are an easy tram ride or a walk away.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the dates, the apartment and the size of your group. Send us those details and we'll send the quote in writing on WhatsApp.

How many people fit in a three-bedroom apartment?

The three-bedroom apartment in Southbank, Melbourne, has six single beds, two to a bedroom, for a group of up to six people. The two-bedroom apartments are for groups of up to four.

What if there are more than six of us?

You can rent more than one apartment, depending on the dates. We have 65 apartments.

Can we rent from Mexico?

Yes, and we have been hosting students on exchange for over 10 years. When you arrive, we meet you and check you into your apartment, to make sure you're comfortable and have everything you need.

What is in the apartment?

The apartment is furnished and bed linen is included. It has a kitchen with a gas stove and dishwasher, a washing machine, a dryer, fast internet, a large-screen TV with Netflix and air conditioning.

Do you rent single rooms?

No, we rent the whole apartment out. So if you have a group of students, please let us know.
