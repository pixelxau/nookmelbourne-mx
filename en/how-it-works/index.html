How it works

Your apartment, sorted before you fly

Message us from Mexico, get a quote, talk to us on Zoom, and book before you fly.

Message us on WhatsApp

  1. You message us

    Send us your arrival and departure dates on WhatsApp, how many people are coming and which size of apartment you're after.

  2. We send options

    We reply with the apartments we have for those dates, how the beds are set up and a quote.

  3. We talk on Zoom

    We get on a Zoom call with your group and answer all your questions about Melbourne, Southbank, the nightlife, university, banking, etc.

  4. You book it

    You book the apartment before leaving Mexico, with the quote in writing in the chat.

  5. You arrive in Melbourne

    We meet you and check you into your apartment on City Road, to make sure you're comfortable and have everything you need.

After you book

Still with you once you reach Melbourne

From the day you book to the day you settle in, you deal with us directly.

Your bank account
When you arrive, we give you information on opening a bank account. You open the account yourself, directly with the bank.
Where to buy groceries
We tell you where to buy food and fresh fruit and vegetables. There is a supermarket across the road and South Melbourne Market is close by.
Mexican ingredients in Melbourne
We tell you where to find Mexican ingredients and tortillas in Melbourne, to cook in your apartment.
Dinner at our place
We invite you and your group to dinner at our home. We cook Mexican food, so you miss the food back home a little less.

Questions

Can we rent from Mexico?

Yes, and we have been hosting students on exchange for over 10 years. When you arrive, we meet you and check you into your apartment, to make sure you're comfortable and have everything you need.

Who helps the students once they are in Melbourne?

We do. We talk on Zoom before they book, meet them on arrival, give them information on opening a bank account, tell them where to buy food and Mexican ingredients, and invite them to dinner at our home.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the dates, the apartment and the size of your group. Send us those details and we'll send the quote in writing on WhatsApp.

How long is a stay?

As long as you want. If you're here for one semester or two, we're flexible, and if you're only here for 3 months, that's fine too.

What is in the apartment?

The apartment is furnished and bed linen is included. It has a kitchen with a gas stove and dishwasher, a washing machine, a dryer, fast internet, a large-screen TV with Netflix and air conditioning.

Do you rent by the room?

No, we rent the whole apartment out. So if you have a group of students, please let us know.

We are a big group. How many apartments do you have?

We have 65 apartments, so a big group can rent more than one, depending on the dates. Send us your dates and how many of you there are.

How early should we book?

As early as you can. Our apartments are booked out every semester, so message us as soon as you have your dates.
