Questions

What beds are in the second bedroom?

The second bedroom has two queen beds or two single beds, depending on what your group prefers. The first has two single beds.

How many people fit in the 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom apartment?

Up to four people, even when the second bedroom has queen beds.

Is the washing machine shared with other apartments?

No. The washing machine and dryer are inside the apartment and only your group uses them.

How far is it from my university?

All the universities are an easy tram ride or a walk away.
